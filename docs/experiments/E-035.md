## Experiment: E-035 — Checklist boxes and claim closure are ungrounded

- **Date:** 02.11.2026
- **Status:** completed
- **Theory:** A checklist record is an audit statement, not a formality: `INV-074` requires each box to be `CHECKED` (with evidence) or `NA` (with a substantive reason), and `INV-036` requires a critical claim closed `VERIFIED` to carry a passing linked test. The gate reads `boxes`, `status` and `reason` but does not require `CHECKED` evidence, accepts any status token, and never ties a critical VERIFIED claim to a passing test. A contract can tick boxes without evidence and close a critical claim on a test that is missing or not passing.

- **Experiment Design:** `scripts/bench/bench_optimization.py --suite checklist` adds four probes: a `CHECKED` record with no `evidence_refs`, a `CHECKED` record rewritten to an unknown status, a critical claim left `VERIFIED` after its only test link is removed, and a prompt probe for the missing box/closure rules. The sixteen earlier suites stay unchanged. `--control clean` must fail the claim.
